- `vga8x16.bin` (4096 bytes: 256 glyphs x 16 rows) is `FONTS/PC-IBM/VGA8.F16`
  from https://github.com/viler-int10h/vga-text-mode-fonts (raw dump of the
  IBM VGA ROM 8x16 text font, preserved by VileR / int10h.org).
